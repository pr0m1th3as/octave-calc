Run GNU Octave analyses from inside your LibreOffice Calc spreadsheet.

Forty-six of them, across four categories, and your custom functions
written in GNU Octave as well. Data > Statistics with GNU Octave opens
one dialog, where each analysis says what it is for and when to use
it instead of its neighbours. Results are written into the sheet as
one undoable action, and nothing is overwritten without asking.

Two spreadsheet functions, OCTAVE and OCTRANGE, call an Octave function
on the cells of your sheet from a formula.

Needs GNU Octave with the devtools (>=0.2.1) and statistics (>=1.9.3)
packages installed. Formulas run only inside a sandbox; the Statistics
menu runs with or without one.
