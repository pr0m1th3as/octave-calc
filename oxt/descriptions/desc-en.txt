Run GNU Octave analyses on the data in your LibreOffice Calc spreadsheet,
without leaving Calc.

Data > Statistics with GNU Octave opens one dialog holding forty-six
analyses in four categories, group comparisons, distribution fitting,
random numbers and experimental design, each saying what it is for and
when to use it instead of its neighbours. A fifth category runs one of
your own Octave functions the same way. Results are written into the
sheet as one undoable action, and nothing is overwritten without asking.

Two spreadsheet functions, OCTAVE and OCTRANGE, call an Octave function on
the cells of your sheet from a formula.

Needs GNU Octave with the devtools and statistics packages installed.
Formulas run only inside a sandbox; the Statistics menu runs with or
without one.
